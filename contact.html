Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I order?

At least 3 days for cupcakes and simple cakes, 1–2 weeks for themed and sculpted cakes, and 1–3 months for wedding cakes. Short notice? Call us and we'll do our best.

How do I pay?

We accept Interac e-Transfer. Pay a 50% deposit to reserve your date and the balance at pickup, or pay in full when you order.

Do you deliver?

Orders are pickup only. We'll share the pickup address and confirm your time once your order is confirmed.

Can I change the design of a cake in the shop?

Absolutely. Add your changes in the order notes at checkout or send us a custom enquiry, and we'll confirm any price difference before baking.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Please tell us about any allergies when you order. Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts, so we can't guarantee an allergen-free environment, but we'll always talk through your options.

How should I store my cake?

Buttercream cakes keep best in the fridge. Bring them out 1–2 hours before serving. Fondant cakes prefer a cool, dry room. See our cake care guide.
