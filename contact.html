Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I order?

At least 3 days for cupcakes and simple cakes, 1–2 weeks for themed and sculpted cakes, and 1–3 months for wedding cakes. Short notice? Call us and we'll do our best.

How much does a cake cost?

Every cake is made to order, so the price depends on the size, design and detail. Send us a quote request with what you have in mind and we'll reply with a personal quote, usually within one business day.

How do I pay?

Once you're happy with your quote, we'll confirm your date and share the payment details (Interac e-Transfer).

Do you deliver?

Orders are pickup only. We'll share the pickup address and confirm your time once your order is confirmed.

Can I change the design of a cake in the shop?

Absolutely. Add your changes to the quote request on that cake's page, or send us a custom enquiry, and we'll include them in your quote.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Please tell us about any allergies when you order. Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts, so we can't guarantee an allergen-free environment, but we'll always talk through your options.

How should I store my cake?

Buttercream cakes keep best in the fridge. Bring them out 1–2 hours before serving. Fondant cakes prefer a cool, dry room. Keep your cake out of direct sun and drive home with it on a flat surface.
