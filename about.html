Hello, we're Cakes Ami

A small kitchen with a big love for cake

Cakes Ami began with a simple belief: a celebration cake should be as personal as the moment it celebrates.

Every cake we make starts with a conversation. Who is it for? What do they love? What colours make them smile? From there we sketch, bake and decorate by hand, whether it's a gentle floral naked cake, a three-tier wedding showpiece or a dinosaur jungle for a five-year-old.

We bake in small batches and to order, so your cake is fresh for your day and made only for you.
